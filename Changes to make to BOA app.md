## **UI changes:**

#### **Overall UI Changes**

The role specification under the user's name is fine but i want to make sure that it is clear which department that user belongs to as well. This is not supposed to just end with the rentals department the project will expand to every department eventually.

There needs to be a role to approve suppliers.

As it is i think everything is also too segregated. There should be people with multiple roles, example the managers should also have to make requisitions to those higher than them in the DOA while also being an approver for those below them in the DOA. The department managers also determine the budget along with the executive leadership team for each department every year so they should be able to have the budget approver role.

Cost centers need to go. It should just be one department budget that keeps the department operations going for now.



#### **Requester Role UI**

The screen with the requisitions for requester role, i want to make the entire bar clickable instead of just the reference number.

I also want to change the status column to show if invoice/quotation is pending, the status can remain but instead of the current status system i want it to say draft if not submitted for approval with quotation, approved if submitted with quote and approved by relevant parties, and completed if invoice is uploaded after approval.

Two new columns need to be added which are the quotations column and the invoice column which directly display when either or both documents have been uploaded and provides a clickable link to download them from the root of the requisitions page instead of having to click the reference number in order to view the quotations and invoices.

When I'm on the reference number screen I want to change the word attachments to quotations/estimate because that's the only attachment that i should be uploading to that field anyways.

#### 

#### **System Admin UI**

The system admin UI needs to be able to add new users to the system with their given roles (I'm thinking a simple form UI with all the relevant details), an approved supplier list (they should be in charge of uploading the approved suppliers list once it's approved by the relevant parties, use your discretion to figure out who those people might be based on the DOA above).





**Notes about current functionality:**

I like the app overall i think its heading down the right path it just needs to tighten up the roles and not be so segregated. I think the approver role should be held by anyone in tier 5 and above and depending on what they have the authority to approve they will be assigned different levels of approval.
I also think that the system admin should be able to have a no code approach to a lot of things with regards to adding people and adding roles. I just want the app to be very comprehensive and easy to use overall. The aim is still fundamentally to act as a logbook of approvals, i just need to tighten up on what is being defined as a separate role. As I see it right now the roles should be:


1. Sys Admin
2. Approver Tier 5 (for supervisors/lowest level managers)
3. Approver Tier 4 (for VP's/Senior Managers)
4. Approver Tier 3 (for executive Board)
5. Accounts
6. Auditor
7. Requester (for all levels)



Now the DOA goes all the way to Tier 1 but tier 2 and tier 1 are above the scope of this app. we are focused on the Subsidiary Group (aka Massy Motors Ltd Trinidad) to start with. Eventually with further development tier 2 and tier 1 will be involved. 



Some features for each tier of approver listed would be as follows:
Tier 5-  should just be for daily operations approvals to spend requested by people in tier 6 up to a certain amount as recommended in the DOA, and for Budget Requests and for Supplier Approval Requests (this means that they should be requesting people in tier 4 to approve their budgets and their suppliers)

Tier 4- should used to approve tier 5 requests to spend up to their recommended amount in the DOA and for approvals of budgets requested by tier 5 approvers, and to approve suppliers requested by Tier 5.

Tier 3- should be used for approvals of tier 4 requests to spend where they need to request permission to spend outside of their limit, and to approve any executive level decisions to be made by tier 4.



As of right now there is one AVP and 3 managers in Leasing so make the number of approvals needed make sense.



For the accounts role:

The head of accounts (or a delegated person within accounts) should be involved in every financial decision being made as an approving person. They have to be the final hurdle for approvals. 



They are the separate body kind of like the SEC where they conform to their own rules. They have no incentive to spend thus making their veto the most important. They should not be requesters, but they should have all the relevant access to all the information minus the SysAdmin's info and be able to approve any financial requests as previously stated.
